# News sources

Euro Info 4.0 reads 92 feeds: European Union affairs and national desks across
Europe, US politics and economy that matter beyond the US, global markets and technology. Every feed was
checked for current entries when the catalogue was built. Country is the publisher's base and only nudges
ranking; it never hides a story. English feeds, and feeds in your interface language, are on by default.
Change the selection in **Manage sources**.

Inclusion is not an endorsement or a reliability rating. *Paywall* means some articles may need a
subscription; *Official* marks an institution's own statements; *Research* marks analysis and commentary.
