# How ranking works

Everything is computed locally and explained in the app: hover a story's score or press **Why featured**.

- **Importance (0–100)** combines how many publisher families and countries cover a story, its subject
  (politics, economy, security, technology), key institutions and people (EU institutions, NATO, central banks,
  heads of government, major companies), freshness (half-life of 20 hours) and official sources.
- **Lower priority**: sport, celebrity, lifestyle and local crime, and US stories without consequences beyond
  the US (no link to Europe, trade, markets, foreign policy, federal institutions or major technology).
  **Focus** hides them; otherwise they simply rank lower.
- **Developing stories** group reports about the same event by shared headline terms within three days. A
  group is a reading aid, not proof that the reports are independent.
- **Topics and regions** are recognised in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Romanian, Polish and
  Dutch. A story's region follows what it is about as well as where the publisher is based.
- **Trending** compares each subject's share of the last six hours with earlier refreshes. A publisher
  mentioning its own country does not count.
- **For you** (optional) adds up to 8 points for stories close to what you open, save and annotate. The
  profile is built on your computer from your reading history and is never sent anywhere.

These are ranking signals, not reliability scores.
